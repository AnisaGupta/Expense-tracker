QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Check these common questions before submitting a query.

How do I place an order?

Browse the available local food businesses, select a food item, add it to your cart and proceed to checkout.

How can I check my order status?

Open the My Orders section from your account menu to view your order details and current order status.

I did not receive my OTP. What should I do?

Check your spam or junk folder first. If the OTP still does not arrive, wait a short while and request a new OTP.

Can I cancel my order?

Cancellation may depend on the current status of your order and the cancellation rules of the selected food provider.
