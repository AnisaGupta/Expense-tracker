Privacy & Policy

Please read the policies and guidelines that apply when using LocalBites.

Privacy Policy

How LocalBites collects and uses your information

1. Information We Collect

When you create and use a LocalBites account, we may collect information such as your name, username, email address, mobile number, delivery address, order details and account preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide LocalBites services, manage your account, process orders, display relevant food providers and improve the overall user experience.

3. Email Verification

LocalBites may use email-based OTP verification during registration and other account-related activities. The OTP helps verify ownership of the email address provided by the user.

4. Location Information

LocalBites may use the location or address provided by you to help display nearby local food businesses and support delivery or pickup-related functionality.

5. Orders and Payments

Information related to your orders may be stored to provide order history and order management. Where online payments are enabled, payment processing is handled through the configured payment service.

6. Account Security

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential. Passwords and OTPs should not be shared with other people.

7. Data Protection

LocalBites is designed to use reasonable measures to protect account and application information. Users should also take appropriate steps to protect their login credentials and personal information.

Terms of Use

Guidelines for using the LocalBites platform

1. Account Information

Users should provide accurate information when creating their LocalBites account and should keep their account information up to date.

2. Responsible Usage

LocalBites should be used only for legitimate food discovery, ordering and related activities. Users should not attempt to misuse the platform or interfere with its operation.

3. Business Listings

Business owners are responsible for providing accurate information about their business, food items, prices, availability and other details displayed on LocalBites.

4. User Reviews

Reviews and ratings should be genuine and related to the user's experience. Users should not submit misleading, abusive or inappropriate content.

Orders & Cancellation

Information about placing and cancelling orders

1. Order Confirmation

Once an order is placed, the order information is shared with the selected food provider for processing. Order status may be updated as the business processes the order.

2. Order Cancellation

Cancellation availability may depend on the current status of an order and the cancellation rules configured for the food provider.

3. Order Information

Users can view relevant order information through their LocalBites account. Order records may be retained to support order history and account management.

User Responsibilities

Responsibilities of LocalBites users

Customers

Customers should provide correct contact and delivery information, use the platform responsibly and verify their order details before placing an order.

Business Owners

Business owners should provide accurate business, menu, pricing and availability information and should keep their listings updated.
